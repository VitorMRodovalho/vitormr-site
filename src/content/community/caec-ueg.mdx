description: >
  Re-established the extinct Centro Acadêmico de Engenharia Civil at
  Universidade Estadual de Goiás (Anápolis campus) as Director General
  of the elected "Chapa RECONSTRUIR" slate, serving two consecutive
  mandates. Led campaigns for a permanent CAEC headquarters ("Cantinho
  E-Civil"), a university cafeteria, and summer courses; authored
  governance documents (bylaws, election rules, meeting minutes);
  produced the Manual do Calouro freshman guide; designed the CAEC
  visual identity; and built and maintained a custom library system
  (Estante/PHP) for the CAEC book collection. During this period, also
  co-founded — alongside the department's academic coordination under
  Prof. Marcus Vinícius Silva Cavalcanti, after whom the CAEC was named
  — the AMEnC (Associação Minerva de Engenharia Civil), an umbrella
  student association operating at a higher organizational level than
  the CAEC, and the ComEnC (Comissão Permanente de Engenharia Civil),
  the standing committee that organized the annual SemanEnC engineering
  weeks; served as a ComEnC member across SemanEnC editions 3 through 6
  (2005–2008) and authored the SemanEnC 5 PHP web-registration and
  certificate-generation system (2007); led engineering-extension
  activities in partnership with academic coordination. In parallel,
  served as the official web designer for the Civil Engineering
  department under a formal UEG subdomain contract, in partnership with
  GR InfoArt — built and maintained three independent web applications
  on engenhariacivil.ueg.br: a Joomla department portal, a separate
  Joomla CAEC site, and the Biblioteca/Estante system.
